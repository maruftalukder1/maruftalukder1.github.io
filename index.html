About me

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student at Northern University Bangladesh who learns by shipping real products. My biggest build, PawMatch, is a live pet-care & adoption platform — realtime messaging and calls, shelter workflows, and an AI symptom checker that helps owners decide how urgently a sick pet needs a vet.

I care about software that is safe by design: the symptom checker's AI can only ever raise the urgency of a case, never lower it — a principle that became the subject of my first research paper, now in preparation for peer-reviewed conference submission.

Alongside my studies I build AI automations with n8n + OpenAI and take freelance work — chatbots, workflow integrations and web apps for small businesses.

04.

Research

First research paper · In preparation

Escalate-Only AI: A Safety-First Hybrid Architecture for Pet Symptom Triage

How do you let a vision model help with a safety-critical decision without ever letting it talk an owner out of an emergency? My paper evaluates a hybrid design — deterministic rules own the baseline, AI observations can only escalate — on a benchmark of 80 guidance-labelled scenarios with a strict development / held-out protocol.
