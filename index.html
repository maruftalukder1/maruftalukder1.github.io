01. About me

I’m a CSE student at Northern University Bangladesh who learns fastest by shipping real things. My biggest build so far is PawMatch — a live pet-care and adoption platform with an AI symptom checker that pairs a transparent rule engine with photo and video analysis, so it can raise an urgency level but never lower one. It’s the system my first research paper is built around.

Alongside it I built Chitthi, a realtime messenger with calls, stories and an in-app AI assistant, and I freelance with AI automation — wiring tools like n8n into workflows that quietly give businesses their hours back.
